Short, practical reads on automation, agents and marketing for growing companies.

Automation / Nov 27, 2026

Which tasks should you automate first?

Start with work that is repetitive, rule-based and done many times a week.

Read more

Good first candidates are the jobs your team already describes as "copy this, paste that": sending invoices, answering the same customer questions, updating a sheet after every sale, chasing follow-ups.

Write the steps down, count how often it happens and how long it takes, then pick the one with the clearest steps. A small, boring win ships fast and builds trust for bigger automation later.

Keep a person in the loop for anything involving money or sensitive decisions until the flow has proven itself.

Agents / Nov 27, 2026

Chatbot, calling agent or email agent: how to choose

Match the agent to where your customers already talk to you.

Read more

If most enquiries arrive on WhatsApp, Instagram or your website, a chatbot answers instantly and captures the lead. If your sales depend on phone conversations, a calling agent can make first-touch and follow-up calls and log the outcome.

If your pain is a crowded inbox, an email agent can sort, draft and follow up under rules you set. Many companies start with one channel, then add the next once the first is running well.

Video / Nov 27, 2026

What AI avatar videos are good for, and what they are not

Great for fast, repeatable explainers and ads. Not a replacement for every kind of video.

Read more

An AI presenter works well for product explainers, short social ads, training clips and the same message in several languages, without a studio or camera crew.

It is a weaker fit for emotional brand films or founder stories where a real face and real setting matter. A sensible mix is real footage for trust, avatar videos for volume and speed.
